# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and cloud engineers preparing for the AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01) exam. They arrive with varying AWS experience, study at all hours (often late at night on personal machines), and want to move efficiently through structured material without friction.

## Product Purpose

A public study companion that transforms markdown lecture content into a navigable, searchable learning interface. Users browse lectures by exam domain, review key points and exam tips at a glance, and track their progress across 5 weighted domains. Success means someone can find the right concept in seconds and leave with a clear mental model.

## Brand Personality

Focused. Clean. Efficient. The site respects the learner's time — no decorative fluff, no onboarding ceremony, no gamification. It feels like a sharp tool, not a course platform. Think Linear's information density with Raycast's speed-to-value.

## Anti-references

- AWS documentation: dense, hard to scan, buried navigation, walls of unbroken text
- Generic SaaS landing pages with gradient hero sections and identical card grids
- Gamified learning platforms with badges, streaks, or cartoon mascots
- Overly minimal sites that sacrifice scannability for whitespace

## Design Principles

1. **Scannable over readable** — key points, exam tips, and gotchas should be visible without deep reading
2. **Structure is navigation** — the 5-domain hierarchy IS the interface; don't invent a separate nav model
3. **Density without claustrophobia** — pack information tightly but with clear visual rhythm
4. **Speed to concept** — from landing to understanding should be under 3 clicks
5. **Night-shift ready** — dark mode is first-class, not an afterthought

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 contrast for text, 3:1 for UI elements)
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
- Dark and light themes with system-preference detection and manual toggle
- Reduced motion support via prefers-reduced-motion
- Semantic HTML for screen reader navigation of lecture structure
